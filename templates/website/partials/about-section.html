About Us

Rooted in Sustainability, Built for Business

Ecoparrot PriPac Pvt Ltd is a leading manufacturer of eco-friendly packaging solutions based in Kerala, India. We specialize in customized paper bags and non-woven bags designed to meet the diverse packaging needs of supermarkets, fashion boutiques, restaurants & cafés, bakeries, corporate companies, event management firms, the healthcare sector, and educational institutions.

Driven by a strong commitment to sustainability, innovation, and quality, we help businesses transition towards environmentally responsible packaging without compromising on durability, functionality, or brand appeal.

Ecoparrot was founded to close a real gap in Kerala's packaging market: most retailers were sourcing paper and non-woven bags from other states, adding cost and delay. We built Kerala's first fully automated bag manufacturing facility so businesses could get premium-quality, custom-branded packaging locally — faster, and at a fairer price.
